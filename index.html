Inside the Algorithm

Ever wondered why a striker with a hat-trick might get a lower rating than a playmaker with no goals? It's all about "Action Density."

The "Action Density" Bias

Sofascore and WhoScored process 2,000+ events per game. A "Poacher" like Haaland might touch the ball 15 times, scoring once. This yields a strong but static rating (~7.5).

Typical Striker Involvement Profile

The Multi-Phase Advantage

A "Playmaker" like Messi or Yamal drops deep, completes progressive passes, and dribbles. Every successful action adds a fractional point. By the time they score, their rating is already hovering near a 9.0.
